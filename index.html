[01] ABOUT

WHO I AM

I am a Telecommunications Engineering student with a strong passion for Embedded Systems, Hardware Design, and RF technologies.

My core engineering focus is bridging physical hardware with low-level software. I design custom PCB layouts, develop firmware in C/C++, and implement real-time systems (FreeRTOS) to interface microcontrollers with sensors and communication protocols.

I enjoy tackling challenging technical projects—from sensorized robotic peripherals to high-frequency wireless nodes—ensuring efficiency, reliability, and precision at every level of hardware-software integration.
